I rebuilt this site from scratch. The old one was a tidy little portfolio, which is a polite way of saying it was boring.

This version has two jobs:

- tell you a little about me
- give me somewhere to write down random thoughts

That's it. No tags, no categories, no content strategy.

## How posting works

Each post is a markdown file in `content/posts/`. Add a file, give it a `title` and a `date`, push to `main`, and it's live.

> Posts may be wrong, half-baked, or both. That's the point.
